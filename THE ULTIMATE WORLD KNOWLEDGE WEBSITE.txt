# THE ULTIMATE WORLD KNOWLEDGE WEBSITE

What you are describing could become something much larger than a course platform.

It would be a **living, visual, interactive map of human knowledge**: mathematics connects to physics, physics to engineering, engineering to computing, computing to AI, AI to biology, biology to medicine, medicine to ethics, ethics to philosophy, philosophy to history, history to economics, economics to politics, and so on.

The core idea:

> **Everything humans know should be discoverable as part of one interconnected knowledge universe.**

Not just “courses.”

Not just “Wikipedia.”

Not just “ChatGPT.”

Not just “Coursera.”

A combination of **Wikipedia + MIT OpenCourseWare + Khan Academy + YouTube + Google + Notion + ChatGPT + a university + a research database + a video game world map**.

And the interface should make learning feel like **exploring a universe**, not browsing a database.
